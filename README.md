# 🌶️ Green Pepper Cup  🌶

> **BRING THE HEAT. OWN THE GAME.**

Green Pepper Cup  is a Counter-Strike 2 offline tournament hosted at **NOVEZERO LAN ,Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal**, from **3–6 December 2026**.

## Competition Format

- **16 teams**.
- **4 groups of 4 teams**.
- Group Stage: **BO1 Round-Robin**.
- Win = **3 points**; Loss = **0 points**.
- The **top 2 teams from each group** advance to the Playoffs.
- Playoffs are **Single Elimination** and played as **BO3**.
- The tournament is played offline/on LAN over four days.
- Match schedules may operate on a rolling basis depending on tournament progress.

## Seeding

Seeding uses the **Valve Regional Standings (VRS)** where available, with alternative criteria for teams without VRS. The tournament Rulebook defines the complete hierarchy and the VRS snapshot date.

## Registration

Registration opens **28 September 2026 at 18:00 CET**.

The first registered teams are selected to participate. Registration is only complete after payment confirmation.

See [`RULEBOOK.md`](RULEBOOK.md) for eligibility, roster changes, equipment requirements, penalties and all competition rules.
