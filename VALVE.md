## VRS Eligibility

Green Pepper Cup  is compatible with the **Valve Regional Standings (VRS)** system.

Teams **do not need existing VRS points** to participate.

The tournament is open to the **Global region**, subject to Valve's applicable regional eligibility rules.

## VRS Seeding

For teams with a VRS ranking:

1. VRS ranking is considered first.
2. The VRS snapshot used for tournament seeding is **2 November 2026**.
3. The relevant VRS core must be maintained to preserve the applicable ranking.
4. Changes to VRS after the snapshot date do not change tournament seeding.

### Alternative Seeding

If a team has no VRS ranking:

1. The **HLTV ranking** from the same date as the VRS ranking may be used.
2. If the team has neither VRS nor HLTV ranking, the average **FACEIT ELO** of its registered players at the end of 2 November 2026 is used.
3. A player without a current FACEIT ELO uses their last known ELO.
4. A player who has never had FACEIT ELO is assigned **1000** for the calculation.

## VRS Core

Teams must maintain the core represented in the VRS ranking used for seeding.

The tournament Rulebook contains the complete roster-change provisions.

## Tournament Format

### Group Stage

- 16 teams.
- 4 groups of 4.
- BO1 Round-Robin.
- Top 2 from each group advance.
- 8 teams qualify for Playoffs.

### Playoffs

- Single Elimination.
- BO3.
- Quarterfinals.
- Semifinals.
- Grand Final.

## Venue

**NOVEZERO LAN**  
Vila Nova de Gaia , Portugal.

All stages are played offline/on LAN.

The tournament administration will operate the event according to its Rulebook and applicable Valve tournament requirements.
