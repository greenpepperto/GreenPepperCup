# GREEN PEPPER CUP I — SCHEDULE

## Tournament Schedule

**Location:** NOVEZERO LAN, Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal

**Dates:** 3–6 December 2026

**Teams:** 8

**Prize Pool:** €5,000

**Game:** Counter-Strike 2

---

## Tournament Format

### Group Stage — GSL / Double Elimination

* 2 GSL Groups
* 4 Teams per Group
* Group Stage uses a Double Elimination / GSL format
* Upper Bracket Matches — BO1
* Lower Bracket Matches — BO3
* 3 teams qualify from each group for the Playoffs
* 1 team is eliminated from each group
* 5 matches per group
* 10 Group Stage matches in total

### Playoffs

* 6 Teams
* Single Elimination
* Quarterfinals — BO3
* Semifinals — BO3
* Grand Final — BO3
* No Third-Place Match

---

## Schedule System

The tournament operates on a **Rolling Schedule due to the Venue Limitations**.

### Group Stage Timing

* Estimated BO1 match block: 45 minutes
* Estimated BO3 match block: 2 hours and 15 minutes
* Technical / team changeover period: 30 minutes
* Lunch / break is scheduled after the completion of each group
* The next match may begin earlier or later depending on the duration of the previous match

### Playoff Timing

* Estimated BO3 match block: 2 hours and 15 minutes
* Technical / team changeover period: 30 minutes
* Match times may move according to tournament progress

Teams must be present **1 hour and 30 minutes before their first scheduled match**.

All teams must remain available according to the rolling schedule.

# SCHEDULE RULES

* **All times listed in this schedule are based on the GMT timezone (UTC+0).**
* **Due to venue and hardware limitations, the tournament operates on a Rolling Schedule basis.**
* **NOVEZERO LAN** in Vila Nova de Gaia is the official tournament venue.
* Only one match can be played at a time due to the available competition setup.
* Match times are estimates and may change depending on tournament progress.
* Matches may begin earlier or later depending on the duration of previous matches and tournament operations.
* Teams must be present at the venue and ready to play according to the rolling schedule.
* Teams must remain available after their first match until they are eliminated from the tournament or their competition day has concluded.
* Teams must be present **1 hour and 30 minutes before their first scheduled match**.
* A **15-minute match grace period** applies.
* Failure to be ready after the grace period may result in a forfeit.
* Group Stage BO1 matches include a planned 30-minute technical and team changeover period between matches.
* Group Stage BO3 matches include a planned 30-minute technical and team changeover period between series.
* Playoff BO3 matches include a planned 30-minute technical and team changeover period between series.
* Lunch / break periods are scheduled after the completion of the respective group.
* Exact match start times remain subject to tournament administration.
* **5 players per team** are expected for each match.
