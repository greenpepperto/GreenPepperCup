## 1. General Provisions

Green Pepper Cup is a Counter-Strike 2 competition held from 3 to 6 December 2026 at NOVEZERO LAN , Vila Nova de Gaia, Portugal.

The competition is open to the Global region in accordance with Valve's regional eligibility rules.

The tournament consists of two offline stages:

1. Group Stage
2. Playoffs

The playing location of each team may be determined through a draw.

Each player must bring their own peripherals, including keyboard, mouse, mousepad, sound card, headset and in-ears. Every game must be played with noise-cancelling headphones and in-ears.

If a player does not have the required equipment and cannot find a solution within 15 minutes of the scheduled match time, the team may be sanctioned with a loss of the relevant match/map.

## 2. Registrations

Teams must complete the official registration form.

Registration is handled by a team representative, who must provide all required information.

- Registration opens: **28 September 2026 at 18:00 CET**
- Registration closes: **8 November 2026 at 13:00 CET**
- Registration fee: **€300 + VAT per team**

The first registered teams will be selected to participate.

Teams will be contacted for payment. Registration is only considered complete after payment has been completed and confirmation has been issued.

If a team fails to meet the payment deadline, its place may be given to a backup team according to the order of form submission.

Team captains must sign the required liability statement regarding damage, misuse, negligence or theft of event equipment.

Only individuals over 13 years of age may register.

### Roster Changes

A maximum of **two player changes** is permitted after roster submission.

- Until 16 days before the event: player replacements may be made subject to administration requirements.
- From 15 to 7 days before the event: roster changes are allowed, but the **VRS core must remain unchanged**.
- Within 7 days of the event: emergency roster changes are only permitted in exceptional circumstances, with valid justification and tournament administration approval.

Teams have a **15-minute grace period** from the scheduled match time. Exceeding the grace period can result in a default loss. In BO3 matches, the sanction applies to the relevant map, with an additional 15 minutes to join and start the next map.

## 3. Tournament Format

### 3.1 Group Stage Scoring

The Group Stage uses a round-robin system.

- Win: **3 points**
- Loss: **0 points**


### 3.2 Group Stage Tiebreakers

If two or more teams finish with the same number of points, the following criteria apply in order:

1. Head-to-head result.
2. Round difference across all group matches.
3. Fewer total round losses across all group matches.
4. Higher initial tournament seed.

### 3.3 Overall Format

- 16 teams.
- 4 groups of 4 teams.
- Group matches: BO1 Round-Robin.
- Top 2 teams from each group advance.
- 8 teams advance to the Playoffs.
- Playoffs: Single Elimination.
- Playoff matches: BO3.
- Tournament duration: 4 days.

## 4. Tournament Schedule

The detailed schedule depends on the final number of registered teams and tournament administration.

The tournament follows a rolling schedule, meaning matches may start earlier depending on administration and the progress of other matches.

Each team must be present for its first match **1 hour and 30 minutes before** the scheduled start time.

## 5. Settings and Maps

### 5.1 Server Configuration

- Round time: **1:55**
- Freeze time: **15 seconds**
- Buy time: **20 seconds**
- Overtime start money: **$12,500**
- Overtime mode: **BO6**
- Agent skins: **Not allowed**
- Map pool:
  - Ancient
  - Cache
  - Dust II
  - Inferno
  - Mirage
  - Nuke
  - Anubis

### 5.2 BO1 Map Veto

1. Team A removes 1 map.
2. Team B removes 1 map.
3. Team A removes 1 map.
4. Team B removes 1 map.
5. Team A removes 1 map.
6. Team B removes 1 map.
7. The remaining map is played.

A knife round determines starting sides. The higher seed decides who starts the ban phase.

### 5.3 BO3 Map Veto

1. Team A removes 1 map.
2. Team B removes 1 map.
3. Team A picks 1 map.
4. Team B chooses its starting side.
5. Team B picks 1 map.
6. Team A chooses its starting side.
7. Team A removes 1 map.
8. Team B removes 1 map.
9. The remaining map is the decider if required.

A knife round is played on the final map to determine starting sides. The higher seed decides who starts the ban phase.

## 6. Withdrawals

Teams may withdraw without penalty only when the withdrawal occurs more than 15 days before the event.

From 15 days before the event onward, a team that withdraws or fails to appear for a scheduled match receives a forfeit loss.

No exceptions are guaranteed after this deadline. Tournament administration decisions are final.

## 7. Round Restoration

Round restoration may only be applied when all of the following conditions are met:

- No more than 60 seconds have passed after freeze time ends.
- No player has taken damage.
- The bomb has not been planted.

## 8. Tactical Pauses

Each team receives:

- 3 tactical pauses per match.
- Each tactical pause lasts 30 seconds.
- 1 additional tactical pause is granted if the match reaches overtime.

The coach may speak only during tactical pauses and side switches.

All players must keep their Steam status offline while playing a match.

## 9. Forbidden Actions

The use of software or hardware that provides an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.

Communication between a participant and anyone not involved in the match is prohibited.

The organization may sanction prize money and/or ban players or teams from future competitions for unsportsmanlike or disrespectful behaviour toward participants, staff or the organization.

## 11. Valve Regional Standings (VRS)

Green Pepper Cup  is compatible with the **Valve Regional Standings (VRS)** system.

Teams are **not required to have VRS points** to participate.

The tournament is open to all regions subject to Valve's general eligibility rules.

Teams without VRS may participate and will be seeded using alternative criteria defined by tournament administration.

For teams with VRS, seeding takes the VRS ranking as of **2 November 2026** into account.

To preserve the relevant VRS position, the team must maintain the core represented in the applicable ranking.

VRS changes after 2 November do not alter tournament seeding.

If teams remain without a seed after VRS is considered, the HLTV ranking from the same date is used.

If a team has neither VRS nor HLTV ranking, the average FACEIT ELO of its registered players at the end of 2 November is used. If a player has no current ELO, their last known ELO is used; if they have never had ELO, **1000** is used.
